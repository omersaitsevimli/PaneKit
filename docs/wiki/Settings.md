# Settings

Menu bar → **Settings…**

## General

- Launch at Login  
- Drag-to-snap / shared-edge resize / Option+green → Layouts  
- Stage Manager inset  
- Restore workspace on display connect  
- Almost-maximize inset, window gap, Pin sidebar width  

## Shortcuts

Click a row to record a new chord, **Reset** for default, ✕ to disable.

## Related menu toggles

Snap zone thickness, gap, Pin Mode, and feature toggles are also on the status-item menu.
